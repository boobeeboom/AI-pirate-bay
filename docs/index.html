What’s the difference between a live cohort and a recording?

A cohort learns together during the month, with two live sessions. A recording is the same module afterwards, all at once. Both cost the same and include the Telegram group and three months of access.

Can I join a cohort after it starts?

Yes, until sales close 5 days in. Missed sessions are recorded.

When does the next live cohort open?

Module 2 sales open in late November. The cohort starts in December.

What if I can’t attend a session?

Watch the recording afterward and work through the material at your own pace.

What language is it in?

Written material and source code are in English. Live group sessions and the Telegram community are in Russian.

How long will I have access?

You can revisit the material, source code, exercises, and recordings for three months after purchase.

Does buying another Module extend my access?

Buying a new Module renews access to all Modules you have acquired for three more months from the purchase, including those whose access has expired.
