We process data on these legal bases:

  • Legal obligations: for accounting, tax, consumer-protection, and similar requirements.
  • Legitimate interests: to operate the service, keep it secure, prevent abuse, and resolve disputes.
  • Consent: to send optional email updates.

Providing data needed for account creation, authentication, purchase, payment, tax records, and access management is required for those features. If you do not provide it, we may not be able to provide the relevant account, purchase, or access functionality.

6. Data Retention

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes above, unless a longer period is required by law.

In practice, this typically means:

Account and access data: kept for the lifetime of your account, or until deletion is requested unless retention is required by law. Purchase and payment records: kept for the period required by accounting and tax law. Security logs, if any: kept for a limited period needed for security and abuse prevention. Email-consent records: kept as needed to respect and demonstrate your consent status, including suppression records after withdrawal.

9. Your Rights

Under the GDPR and other applicable laws, you may have these rights:

  • Access: ask what personal data we process about you.
  • Rectification: correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erasure or restriction: ask us to delete data or restrict processing.
  • Portability: receive your data in a structured format where applicable.
  • Objection: object to processing based on legitimate interests, especially direct marketing.
  • Complaint: lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

Where required, we notify recipients of rectification, erasure, or restriction requests.

Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw consent at any time. Email-update consent can also be withdrawn through your Profile.

The supervisory authority in Austria is the Austrian Data Protection Authority (Datenschutzbehoerde): https://www.dsb.gv.at/.

10. Automated Decision-Making
